What the critique found, and what changed

A haze with no source

An orange glow sat in the corner of every dark card, and orange button shadows bled onto navy. Light should come from somewhere; this read as a smudge. Removed, so the dark surfaces are clean and the stars do the atmosphere.

Home was a card on a page

Mini Me was boxed into a card, under a name that sat outside it. Now its sky runs edge to edge under the status bar, as on the welcome screen, and the greeting, Mini Me and the score share one stage. One fewer container, a bigger character, and the status bar turns white over the sky and frosted once it scrolls away. (Figure–ground, consistency, Nielsen #4.)

Rows were sliced off by the tab bar

Content stopped at a hard white slab. The tab bar is now a translucent material that content slides under, like iOS, so the page feels continuous. (Platform conventions, Jakob’s law.)

Learn looked like a settings list

Stretches were tiny icons while lessons had pictures, and a separate streak card measured a list it wasn’t attached to. Stretches now have illustrations in the lesson style, and the streak target is the list’s own header, the same ring header as Home’s plan. (Similarity, proximity, consistency.)

Friends were initials

Classmates now appear as their own Mini Me, coloured and postured by their score, so the social tab lives in the same world as the rest of the app.

Small things that added noise

Check-in choices lost the box inside the box, and the pain row shows a real prompt (“0 is no pain, 10 is the worst”) instead of a lone dash. Three “Free” chips under a “Free” header became tick circles you tap. Progress numbers are ink, with colour moved to small icons. “Start the safety check” is a normal primary button: red stays for real warnings. The pain map sits in the middle of the screen, and the specialists title fits on one line. The focus ring uses the brand colour.

Kept from earlier iterations

One focal action per screen, actions in the thumb zone, Mini Me’s growth, the Night and Sunrise palette, colour-coded activities, transitions, skeleton/empty/error states, offline queue, in-context permissions, Dynamic Island feedback, 44pt targets, reduced-motion support.
